# Abhyas Logo System

## Assets

- `public/assets/logo-mark.svg`: square geometric A mark for compact placements.
- `public/assets/logo-full.svg`: mark and Abhyas wordmark for email and social previews.
- `public/assets/favicon.svg`: dark-background favicon variant.

The mark combines an ascending step with an A silhouette to represent practice and progress. It uses the brand yellow (`#FFD60A`) against the site's deep navy (`#000814`) and has no external font or image dependencies.

The Navbar and Footer use `logo-mark.svg`; email templates, payment checkout, and social metadata use `logo-full.svg`. Frontend brand text and tagline are defined in `src/config/brand.ts`.
